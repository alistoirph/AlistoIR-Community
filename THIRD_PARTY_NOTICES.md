# Third-party notices

Original third-party notices are retained in the installer runtime. This is an initial inventory, not a complete license audit.

| Bundled component | Notice location in installed runtime |
|---|---|
| Leaflet | `assets/vendor/leaflet/LICENSE` |
| PHPGangsta GoogleAuthenticator | `vendor/phpgangsta/googleauthenticator/GoogleAuthenticator-master/LICENSE.md` |

Ubuntu packages (including PHP, MariaDB, Nginx and Python) are installed from the configured operating-system repositories and retain their respective licenses. Browser libraries loaded from external locations are separate dependencies; their availability and licensing still require review.

Do not remove or replace third-party copyright/license text when preparing later releases. No claim is made that this list covers every transitive/browser dependency.
